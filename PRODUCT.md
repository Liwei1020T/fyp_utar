# Product

## Register

product

## Users

StringSence serves badminton players choosing strings and booking restringing services, plus a single shop administrator handling counter, inventory, booking, payment, and service operations. Players often use the app quickly on a phone before play or while visiting the shop. The administrator needs compact, dependable task flows during daily operations.

## Product Purpose

StringSence brings evidence-based string recommendations, booking and service tracking, player communication, and shop operations into one coherent mobile workspace. Success means users can understand the next action, complete it without ambiguity, and trust that every status and value reflects persisted backend data.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trustworthy, and agile. The interface should feel focused and calm while retaining the energy of badminton, with plain language and confident task guidance.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboards, decorative metric cards, gratuitous gradients, excessive animation, glass effects, and visual novelty that obscures standard controls. Do not imitate generic AI products or present synthetic analytics, prices, counters, or operational states.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious.
2. Show real state, not reassuring fiction.
3. Keep player flows approachable and admin flows operationally compact.
4. Reuse familiar controls and consistent interaction vocabulary.
5. Explain constraints only where they change the user's decision or recovery path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across supported mobile and web surfaces. Preserve keyboard and screen-reader access, visible focus, non-color state cues, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, resilient text scaling, and touch targets large enough for reliable one-handed use.
